Add these lines to your existing .env.local file:

SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
SMTP_PORT=465
SMTP_SECURE=true
SMTP_USER=j3crentalproperties@gmail.com
SMTP_PASS=PASTE_GOOGLE_APP_PASSWORD_HERE
INQUIRY_TO=j3crentalproperties@gmail.com

For SMTP_PASS, use the 16-character Google App Password, NOT the normal Gmail password.
Do not commit .env.local to Git.

For the live website, add the same values in your hosting provider's Environment Variables / Secrets and redeploy.
